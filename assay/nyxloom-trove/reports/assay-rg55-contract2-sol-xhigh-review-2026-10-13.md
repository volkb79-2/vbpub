### Cgroup contract and remaining acceptance

The **shared-slice attribution design is sound in source for sibling containers**, subject to live proof. The observer authenticates a peer and reads its cgroup path and ancestors. Assay counts the candidate leaf’s limit events, ignores ancestor *hierarchical-only* increases attributable to siblings, and refuses an ancestor *local* event when the candidate leaf did not hit its limit ([resource_limits.py](/workspaces/vbpub/.worktrees/cgprofile-cp20-contract2-20261012/assay/src/assay/resource_limits.py:328), [resource_observer.py](/workspaces/vbpub/.worktrees/cgprofile-cp20-contract2-20261012/scripts/cgroup-profiler/lib/resource_observer.py:379)). This is attribution to the lane container during a serial candidate window, not exclusive accounting for a candidate process. CP-23’s per-candidate cgroups would provide stronger isolation but are substantial future work, not a prerequisite for this serial contract.

That is a **live hypothesis**, not a finding of observed refusals on this tree. B110’s recorded pilot stopped before candidate measurement, and B131’s prior pilot evidence cannot establish current readiness. Neither supports a GO claim yet.
