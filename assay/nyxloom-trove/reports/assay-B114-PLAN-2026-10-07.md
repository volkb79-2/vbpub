### The required P3b correction

A declared fallback may report `killed` only when its receipt proves a **call-phase test failure**: matching coverage-baseline collection and fingerprints, a valid started prefix reaching the failed node, consistent process and session exit 1, and no collection, setup, teardown, auxiliary, signal, termination, or cgroup-event cause. The declared command’s known pytest-cov wrapper may make the receipt `unsupported`; allow that *specific pinned hook condition* without weakening the ordinary B106 witness rule.

If a positive exit lacks that proof, classify it as `crashed`/`EXEC_FAILED` (or use the existing whole-lane infrastructure refusal when that is the actual cause). Do not retry beyond the single declared attempt. Put paired negative and positive oracles in `tests/core/test_b114_cold_witness.py` and real-child cases in `tests/core/test_b114_cold_witness_real_runs.py`: collection failure after uncertain cold, setup/teardown failure, invalid or missing receipt, signal, and resource event **never kill**; a proven declared test-call failure **does kill**. Keep a non-cold regression oracle.
